\chapter{Answers to the Exercises}
\label{app:answers}

This appendix answers the exercises that have one right answer: every \kindtag[fillaccent3]{Calculation}
and \kindtag[fillaccent2]{Reflection} exercise, and the questions that some
\kindtag{Code} exercises ask about the design they make you write. Calculations are shown with
their working, so that an answer worked on paper can be checked step by step, not just at the end.
Where an exercise asks you to explain something in your own words, the answer is one way of saying
it, and yours can differ in wording as long as it says the same things.

The Code exercises have no printed solution. Each describes what a program must do on the board,
and the board, or the simulator of Appendix~\ref{sim:ch}, is how you check it.
